Safety & wellbeing

Built for learning, designed for safety

The question every parent asks first — "will my child be safe?" — deserves a very specific answer.

Secured single entry

One monitored entrance with keypad access and a staffed front desk. All visitors sign in and are accompanied on campus.

CCTV & sign-in/out

Cameras cover every common area and entrance, and digital sign-in/out means we always know exactly who is on site — and who collected your child.

Vetted, first-aided staff

Every adult on campus is background-checked, reference-verified and pediatric first-aid certified. Our school nurse is on site daily.

Spotless & air-filtered

Classrooms are cleaned twice daily with child-safe products, and every room has HEPA air filtration and fresh-air ventilation.

Health & allergy care

Individual allergy plans for every child who needs one, nut-aware menus, and staff trained in epinephrine and asthma response.

Supervised everywhere

Children are never unsupervised — indoors, outdoors, on expeditions or at pick-up. Ratios are maintained even on the playground.

Pictures are nice. A visit is better.

Come and walk these rooms yourself — tours every Tuesday & Thursday at 9:30am, or book a private slot that fits your family.
