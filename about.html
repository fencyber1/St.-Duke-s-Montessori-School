Our mission

To raise children who love learning, trust themselves and care for others

We believe education is not the filling of a bucket, but the lighting of a fire. Everything at St. Duke's — the prepared classrooms, the patient guides, the unhurried mornings — exists to protect a child's natural curiosity and let it become mastery.

"The child is both a hope and a promise for mankind. Our job is simply to prepare the environment, then step back and watch greatness unfold." — Adapted from Dr. Maria Montessori, our founding inspiration
Our values

Four values your child will live every day

Respect

For self, for others, for the environment. Grace & courtesy are practiced, not just preached.

Curiosity

Questions are treasures. We follow each child's interests until "I wonder" becomes "I found out!"

Independence

"Help me do it myself" — from tying shoelaces to managing a weekly work plan, step by step.

Community

Mixed-age classrooms mean older children mentor younger ones — and families become lifelong friends.

Our story

From six children around a kitchen table to a campus full of dreams

1989

A kitchen table and a big idea

Founder Margaret "Duke" Ellison, a newly AMI-certified teacher, welcomes six children into her home classroom on Duke Street — named for the street that gave us our name.

1995

The first real classrooms

After a waiting list grew to 90 families, the community rallies to build our first purpose-built Children's House classrooms on Duke Gardens Avenue.

2004

Elementary program opens

Families asked us not to stop at Kindergarten — so we didn't. Lower and Upper Elementary programs open, serving children through age 12.

2013

Full national accreditation

St. Duke's earns its first full accreditation from the National Montessori Council — renewed, with commendation, in 2019 and 2024.

2019

Toddler Community & gardens

Our youngest friends (18 months+) get a beautiful sunlit building of their own, plus teaching gardens and an orchard the children tend themselves.

2026

The STEM & Makerspace Wing

Our newest chapter: a junior science lab, robotics studio and design workshop — Montessori hands, modern tools, limitless curiosity.
