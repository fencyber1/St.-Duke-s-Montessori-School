18 months – 3 years
First beginnings

Toddler Community

Max 12 children · 2 guides Half-day 8:00–12:00 or full-day to 3:30 Potty-learning friendly

These are the miracle years: language explodes, personalities bloom, and little hands ache to do things "by myself." Our toddler environment is soft, slow and perfectly child-sized, so every day ends with a proud "I did it!"

  • Practical life: pouring, dressing, food prep
  • Language: songs, stories, rich vocabulary
  • Movement: climbing, balancing, carrying
  • Sensorial exploration & early art
  • Gentle toilet-learning partnership
  • Daily outdoor garden time

3 – 6 years (incl. Kindergarten)
The Montessori heartland

Children's House (Casa)

Max 24 children · AMI lead + assistant Full day 8:00–3:30 Kindergarten year included

The famous three-year cycle where children typically learn to read, write and do four-digit math — not through worksheets, but through beautiful hands-on materials they choose themselves. The Kindergarten year crowns it all with leadership.

  • Reading & writing with phonics materials
  • Golden-bead math to the thousands
  • Geography, botany & cultural studies
  • Grace, courtesy & conflict resolution
  • Music, art & gardening weekly
  • Kindergarten leadership & mentoring

6 – 9 years
Big questions begin

Lower Elementary

Max 24 children · 2 guides Full day 8:00–3:30 Weekly STEM lab sessions

Six-to-nine-year-olds are powered by two engines: imagination and fairness. Our "cosmic education" curriculum feeds both — through great stories of the universe, collaborative research, and real responsibility for their community.

  • Advanced reading, writing & grammar
  • Multiplication, division & geometry
  • Botany, zoology, earth & physical science
  • History told through great stories
  • Research, presentation & teamwork
  • Weekly work-plan independence
Apply for Lower Elementary
9 – 12 years
Ready for anything

Upper Elementary

Max 24 children · 2 guides Full day 8:00–3:30 Student-run micro-economy

The launchpad years. Upper Elementary students run a real micro-business, publish research, lead campus service projects and take overnight expeditions — graduating with the academic depth and quiet confidence secondary schools notice immediately.

  • Essay craft, debate & public speaking
  • Fractions, decimals, pre-algebra
  • Robotics, coding & design thinking
  • Civics, economics & world cultures
  • Entrepreneurship via the school shop
  • Capstone expedition & portfolio

A day at St. Duke's

What your child's Tuesday looks like

7:30

Warm welcome & early drop-off

Greeted by name at the gate. Quiet activities, breakfast club and gentle starts for early birds.

8:00

The 3-hour Montessori work cycle

Uninterrupted, self-chosen work with individual and small-group lessons. This protected deep-focus time is the heartbeat of our school.

11:30

Family-style lunch

Hot, freshly cooked lunch eaten in small groups with real plates and polite conversation — children set and clear their own tables.

12:30

Outdoor play, garden & orchard

An hour of fresh air every day: climbing, soccer, gardening, or simply cloud-watching in the hammock corner.

1:30

Specialist sessions & clubs

Spanish, music, art, PE, STEM lab or library — the afternoon brings specialist teachers and plenty of laughter.

3:30

Pick-up — or after-care till 5:30

Hugs and stories at the gate for some; after-care clubs, homework help and snacks for our full-day families.
