The St. Duke's standard

What makes our teachers different

  • AMI or AMS certification for every lead guide — 500+ hours of rigorous Montessori training
  • Ongoing mentorship: 40+ hours of professional development every year, observed and coached
  • Background-checked, first-aid certified, and safeguarding-trained annually
  • They stay: 94% retention means no "new teacher again?" conversations in September
  • They know your child — strengths, quirks, worries and all. Ask them; they'll tell you stories.
