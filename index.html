About Our School

We are one of the most loved small schools in Ohio, serving over 400 children across four Montessori programs — from first steps at 18 months to confident graduation at 12 years.

St. Duke's was founded by Margaret "Duke" Ellison in 1989 around a single kitchen-table classroom, for the public benefit of Greenville's children. Though much has grown since, we are still a school that knows every child's name. As a leader in early education, St. Duke's has pioneered calm, child-led learning in our region for 35 years — and parents rate us 4.9 out of 5 for a reason.
