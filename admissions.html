How enrollment works

From curious parent to confident "yes" in 4 steps

No entrance exams, no interview panels, no pressure. Just a warm process designed around your child.

1

Book a tour

Walk our classrooms while they're in session and meet our guides. Tours run Tuesdays & Thursdays at 9:30am — or book a private slot.

~45 minutes
2

Apply online

Complete the simple form below — child's details, preferred program and start date. A one-time $50 application fee confirms your place in line.

~10 minutes
3

Family visit

A relaxed conversation with our principal while your child explores a classroom. For ages 5+, a friendly half-day visit with their future class.

~1 hour
4

Offer & welcome

Offer letter within 5 school days. Secure the place with a refundable $300 deposit (credited to tuition) and join our new-family welcome evening.

Decision in 5 days

What you'll need to apply

  • Child's birth certificate or passport (copy)
  • Up-to-date immunization records
  • Recent report from current school or daycare (ages 5+)
  • One recent photo of your child
  • $50 application fee (waived at Open House)

Can't gather everything right away? No problem — documents can follow your application within 14 days.
